Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Exit Permit Application is designed to streamline employee exit permit verification and security monitoring processes. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the App.

About the App

The Exit Permit helps security personnel monitor and verify employee exits and entries. With real-time updates and an intuitive interface, the App enhances workplace security and operational efficiency by ensuring employee movements are accurately tracked and recorded.

Information We Collect

The App may collect the following information:

  • Employee identification information (such as employee ID, name, or badge number).
  • Exit permit details required for verification.
  • Date and time of employee entry and exit activities.
  • User account information for authorized security personnel.
  • System logs necessary for auditing, troubleshooting, and security monitoring.

How We Use Information

The collected information is used to:

  • Verify employee exit permits and access authorization.
  • Track and record employee movements for security purposes.
  • Provide real-time updates to authorized personnel.
  • Maintain system security and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Improve application performance and reliability.

Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

Data Sharing

  • To comply with legal obligations or lawful requests from authorities.
  • With authorized company systems that support security and operational processes.

User Access and Permissions

The App may require certain permissions necessary for its operation. Any permissions requested are used solely to provide the intended functionality and improve security operations.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill operational, security, auditing, and regulatory requirements.
