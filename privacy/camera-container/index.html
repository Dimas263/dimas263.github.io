Privacy Policy

Camera Container Application

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

Camera Container is an operational mobile application used to capture, manage, store, and submit container activity photographs for business documentation and reporting purposes.

1. Information We Collect

Camera Images

The application uses your device camera to capture photographs related to container operations, loading activities, cargo conditions, inspections, and operational reporting.

Photos and Storage Access

Camera Container may access local storage to:

  • Save photos captured through the application.
  • Store draft images when uploads fail.
  • Access gallery images selected by the user.
  • Maintain image submission history.

Operational Data

The application may store operational information such as:

  • Date and time of image capture.
  • Selected work area or location.
  • Submission status and upload logs.
  • Technical error information used for troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through Camera Container is used solely for operational and business purposes, including:

  • Container operation documentation.
  • Loading and unloading verification.
  • Photo-based reporting.
  • Draft recovery for failed image submissions.
  • System monitoring and troubleshooting.
  • Improvement of application performance and reliability.

3. Data Sharing

Camera Container does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Captured operational data and images are only transmitted to authorized company systems responsible for operational monitoring, documentation, and reporting.

4. Data Retention

Images and operational records may be stored:

  • Temporarily on the user's device.
  • Within application drafts until successfully uploaded.
  • On authorized company servers after successful submission.

Data retention periods follow applicable business and operational requirements.

5. Permissions Used

Camera Permission

Required to capture operational photographs.

Photos / Storage Permission

Required to save images, manage drafts, access gallery photos, and display submission history.

Internet Permission

Required to upload images and synchronize information with company servers.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are implemented to protect stored and transmitted information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Children's Privacy

Camera Container is intended for authorized employees and operational personnel. The application is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

8. Application Features

  • Photo capture and documentation.
  • Failed upload recovery using Draft functionality.
  • Gallery image selection.
  • Operational image history.
  • Area-based photo management.
  • Improved camera functionality and upload reliability.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any modifications will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
